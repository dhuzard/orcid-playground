Follow, Contact, Group, Team: what’s the difference?
Follow
See the outputs a refresh newly finds in their public ORCID record, in your Feed. Nothing is sent to them.
Contact
Your own record of someone you know: context tags, date met, private notes and follow-ups. It stays in this browser and is never part of a team pack or a group link.
Group
A working group you curate: roster, local roles, project outputs and a team pack. Membership is your own note; the people listed are not notified and have not confirmed it.
Team
Membership each person confirms by signing in. That needs cloud Teams, which are not available on this site yet.

FIRST STEPS

Explore & Play, step by step

  1. Open a profile. (read from this site, no ORCID request), or enter any ORCID iD above.
  2. Look around. The trading card, publication heatmap, Wrapped and Record check are tabs of the result.
  3. Export what you need. Each tab has its own download: an SVG card, a record check report, citations.
  4. Keep the researcher, if you like. Save as contact, Add to group or Follow open My Network, and each asks first.

For the people you work with, Connect & Organize in My Network has its own step-by-step guide and an optional labelled sample.

MULTI-ORCID

Bring your working group together.

Follow researchers and see their new outputs in one feed. Build a working group from a pasted list of ORCID iDs, pick the project's outputs, track requests, and download a team pack with the roster, a deduplicated bibliography and every member's biosketch.

Your network is stored only in this browser. Group membership and roles are curated by you and are not confirmed by the people listed.

Uses the public ORCID API. Outputs only re-express what a record asserts; a valid ORCID iD is not proof of identity, and records may be incomplete or out of date.

Everything runs in your browser. The default research network is browser-local; optional cloud Teams and account sync are not yet enabled on the public site and, if activated later, would store team data in a separate EU cloud database.
