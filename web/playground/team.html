Team workspaces

A private page for each team. Only people you invite, who sign in with their own ORCID iD and accept, can open it.

Team data (roles, contributions, notes, requests, selected outputs) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU and is readable only by the team's active members. Public ORCID records are read live and are not copied. Leaving a team removes your access; you can delete anything recorded about you.
