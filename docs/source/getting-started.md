# Get started — no installation

The fastest route to understanding the tool is to **try a reproducible example**, then use your own public ORCID record.

## Option A — try the Studio without sharing anything

1. Open [Studio](https://science-record-player.web.app/).
2. Select **The maintainer's profile (stored, offline)**. It does not make a public ORCID request for that stored example.
3. Inspect the **Profile** and **Record check** tabs.
4. Download a **BibTeX** or **JSON-LD** file from **Citations & data**.
5. Open **Heatmap** or **Wrapped** and compare its summary to what appears in the public record.
6. Under the result, **Keep this researcher in My Network** offers **Save as contact**, **Add to group** or **Follow**. Each opens My Network with the iD filled in and asks before anything is read from ORCID or saved.

## Option B — use your public ORCID iD

1. Find your 16-character ORCID iD on [orcid.org](https://orcid.org/), or copy your record URL.
2. Paste it into the Studio form and select **Explore**. No sign-in is needed to read a public record.
3. Compare the results with your ORCID record. Missing works or affiliations may be non-public, absent, or out of date.
4. Use Record check to decide which changes should be made **in ORCID**.

Your ORCID record is not modified by this tool.

## Option C — explore a group

1. Open the [sample research network](https://science-record-player.web.app/network/?sample=1#groups), and accept the sample-data confirmation. If this browser already holds your own network, the question says so; the sample is added next to it, never in its place, and **Remove sample data** takes it out again. My Network's **Guide** walks through tagging a contact, a private note, a follow-up, a group and a team pack.
2. Open **Groups**, select a demonstration group and inspect its roster, selected outputs, requests, and team-pack export.
3. Visit [Group view](https://science-record-player.web.app/group/) to compose a shareable link from real public ORCID iDs. This public link intentionally does not include private notes, local group roles or selected outputs.

Sample people and outputs are labelled **Demo**. They must not be cited as real scientific records.
