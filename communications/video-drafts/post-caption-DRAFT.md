## Short cut (42 s, square, muted autoplay)

What if your research record had a Play button?

Science Record Player turns one public ORCID record into a scientific trading card, a publication heatmap, a year in
review, a record check with concrete fixes, and citation files (BibTeX, RIS, CSL JSON).

Everything is re-expressed from what the public record asserts. The playful outputs describe activity; they are not a
measure of quality, impact or merit, and they do not verify identity, authorship or affiliations.

The video uses a stored copy of the maintainer's public record, labelled on screen, recorded from a local build.

Open source, no account needed to explore public records: science-record-player.web.app
