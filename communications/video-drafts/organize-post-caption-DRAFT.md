## Short cut (65 s, square or 16:9, muted autoplay)

Remember that scientist you met?

My Network in Science Record Player keeps the context: import people by their public ORCID iD, tag them with the
meeting, write a private note, plan a follow-up with a due date, then turn contacts into a small working group and
download a team pack with the roster, bibliography and biosketches.

- Your notes stay in this browser by default. They are never part of the team pack.
- No invitation or notification sent: the people you add are not contacted.
- Curated membership/project attribution is not independently verified. A group is your own list, not a confirmed team.
- No account needed. There is no cloud sync or e-mail in this workflow.

The researchers in the video are fictional and labelled “(Demo)”, recorded from a local build.

Try it: science-record-player.web.app/network/
